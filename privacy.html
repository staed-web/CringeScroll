Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2025

Welcome to CringeScroll ("we", "us", or "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our website and mobile web app (collectively, the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

a) Account Information

When you sign up, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Username (2–15 alphanumeric characters)
  • Password (securely hashed)

b) Usage & Interaction Data

We automatically collect:

  • Memes you post, react to, tip, or save
  • Chat messages you send in CringeChat
  • Duel activity and outcomes
  • Quest completions and inventory unlocks
  • Timestamps of activity (for streak tracking)

c) Device & Log Data

We collect standard technical information:

  • IP address (via Firebase)
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Error logs

d) Content You Upload

When you create or remix memes:

  • Image URLs (external or data URLs from canvas exports)
  • Meme captions and text overlays
  • Collection names you create
Note: We do **not** store full image files unless you use Fabric.js export (then stored as data URL in Firestore).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the Service
  • To manage your account and CringeCoin balance
  • To display memes, leaderboards, and chat in real time
  • To detect and prevent abuse (e.g., spam, fake accounts)
  • To personalize your experience (e.g., streak rewards, daily quests)
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We **do not sell** your personal data.

We may share data only in these cases:

  • With Firebase (Google): As our backend provider, Firebase processes data per Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • With AdSense: Anonymous ad requests for monetization (no personal identifiers shared).
  • Legal compliance: If required by law or to protect our rights.

4. Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active. You may:

  • Delete your account anytime via Profile → Security → Delete Account
  • Upon deletion, all personal data (username, email, memes, chat history) is permanently erased from Firestore within 30 days

5. Children’s Privacy

CringeScroll is **not intended for users under 13**. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we become aware of such data, we will delete it promptly.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data
  • Export your data
  • Opt out of non-essential data processing

To exercise these rights, contact us at: cringescroll.contact@gmail.com

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures:

  • Firebase Authentication (email/password)
  • Firestore Security Rules (role-based access)
  • HTTPS encryption in transit
  • No plain-text password storage

However, no internet transmission is 100% secure. Use at your own risk.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Continued use after changes implies acceptance.
